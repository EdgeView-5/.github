## What is EdgeView?

EdgeView is an image viewer for macOS from DONGJIN HAN, distributed through the Mac App Store. Its purpose is narrow and it stays there: open image files quickly, browse folders without waiting, and read compressed archives without extracting them first. It is not an editor and does not pretend to be one — there are no adjustment layers, no retouching, no catalogue.

The application is a universal binary, so it runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel Macs without Rosetta.

## Folders with thousands of images

Speed is the whole argument. EdgeView loads directories with several thousand files and moves between them without the pause that makes Preview frustrating at that scale. A built-in browser sits alongside the image, switching between list and icon views, with a thumbnail preview pane, sorting, search, and the ability to rename or delete files without leaving the window.

Zoom controls cover the cases that matter for detailed work: free zoom, fit to window, fit to width, true one-to-one pixels, and a loupe for inspecting a small region without changing the overall view.

## Archives that open in place

Compressed files are opened directly rather than extracted. ZIP, RAR, CBZ, CBR and 7-Zip archives are read as if they were ordinary folders, including nested and password-protected ones. Nothing is written to disk, so browsing a large archive leaves no temporary copies behind and costs no free space.

For anyone keeping scanned material, this changes the daily routine — no unpack, no read, no delete cycle.

## Comics, manga and webtoons

Reading modes go beyond a single page at a time. EdgeView displays two-page spreads for scanned books, handles right-to-left paging for manga, and offers continuous vertical scrolling for webtoons, which are drawn as one long strip rather than discrete pages. Animated files play back too — GIF, APNG and WebP — with frame-by-frame stepping when you want to examine a specific moment.

## Formats Preview turns away

Coverage extends well past the common types. Alongside JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP and TIFF, EdgeView opens PSD, WebP, HEIC, JPEG-XL, AVIF, TGA, OpenEXR and HDR, camera RAW files including DNG, ARW and CR2, and PDF and AI documents. An EXIF panel and histogram sit beside the image, and files or whole folders can be rated.

## Network shares and Finder

Remote volumes are reachable from inside the application over AFP, SMB and FTP, without mounting them in Finder first. A Quick Look extension also teaches macOS itself to show thumbnails and previews for archive files, so the benefit shows up in Finder windows where EdgeView is not even open.

## How to get EdgeView on Mac

1. Install the application and open it from the Applications folder.
2. Drag a folder of images onto the window, or open one from the file browser.
3. Switch the browser between list and icon view to suit the material.
4. Open a comic archive directly — extraction is unnecessary.
5. Set the reading mode for the content: spreads, right-to-left, or continuous scroll.
6. Add a network share over SMB or AFP if your library lives on a NAS.
